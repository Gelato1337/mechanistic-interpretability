Topic features by content domain

Gemma 3 27B with the Gemma Scope 2 262k SAEs on the residual stream, layers 31 to 59. A topic feature belongs to one content domain, its home: the home holds at least half of the prompts the feature fires in, the feature fires in 25 to 300 of the home's 500 prompts and in at most 5 % of all prompts, it fires over spans of a home text (at least 4 tokens and 3 % of the text on average), and it also fires in at least two prompts of each of five other domains. The 36 features below were read by hand.

Each block gives the home domain, the other domains it reaches, a chart of the prompts it fires in per domain, then two home passages and up to two passages from each other domain, with the fired tokens highlighted (darker = stronger; hover for the value). The one-line reading under each feature is the author's, written after reading the passages.
